## 🎯 Clinical Problem & Impact

Patients with Type 2 Diabetes experience high postprandial glycemic excursions that lead to long-term microvascular complications. Traditional management is retrospective. 

**GlucoTwin AI** delivers a prospective digital twin that models individual glucose kinetics:
- **Prospective Forecasting:** Anticipates hyper- and hypoglycemic events 120 minutes in advance.
- **Clinician & Patient Dual View:** Toggles between intuitive lifestyle guidance and clinical biomarker telemetry (Time-in-Range, Glycemic Variability CV%).
- **Interactive Lifestyle Titration:** Simulates carbohydrate loads, physical activity, and rapid insulin boluses in real time.
- **Direct WhatsApp Dispatch:** Generates formatted clinical consultation summaries for instantaneous physician communication.
